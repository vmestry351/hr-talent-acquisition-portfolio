# Recruitment Funnel Dashboard

An Excel workbook that tracks candidates from application to joining and compares sourcing channels and roles.

**File:** `Recruitment_Funnel_Dashboard.xlsx`

## Sheets
- **Dashboard:** KPI cards, hiring funnel, performance by source and role, charts and insights that update automatically.
- **Candidate_Data:** 100 mock candidates (blue cells are inputs; Days to Hire and Final Status are formulas).
- **ReadMe:** how to use the workbook and the assumptions.

## Metrics
Time to hire = joining date minus application date. Offer acceptance rate = joined / offers made. Joining rate by source = joined / applicants from that source. Stage conversion = candidates reaching a stage / candidates in the previous stage.

## What the mock data shows
Employee referrals had the highest joining rate (6 of 14 applicants, about 43%) and the fastest hiring time (about 17 days vs about 27 overall). LinkedIn brought the most applicants but a lower joining rate. These are mock results, not real hiring data.

## Use your own data
Replace the blue cells in `Candidate_Data` with your own **anonymised** data (IDs only, no names or phone numbers).
